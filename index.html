Purpose

Turning raw footage into actionable awareness.

Traditional CCTV only records — someone still has to watch it. SentinelAI adds an intelligent layer on top of your existing cameras so every frame is analyzed the moment it is captured.

The system helps security teams monitor environments at scale, detect violations the second they happen, deliver timely alerts to the right people, and maintain organized incident records for review and compliance.

How It Works

From monitoring to organized records.

Six connected stages run continuously — every second of every day.

01

Monitoring

Cameras capture continuous live footage across every monitored zone.

02

AI Detection

Neural models analyze each frame and flag suspicious behaviour instantly.

04

Core Features

Built for intelligent security.

Everything needed to monitor, detect, alert and document — in one system.

Real-Time AI Detection

Deep-learning models scan every frame at full frame-rate to identify violations as they occur — not minutes later.

Instant Smart Alerts

Authorized personnel are notified within a second through the dashboard, mobile push or email — with the clip attached.

Multi-Camera Coverage

Scale from a single entrance to dozens of zones. Every feed is analyzed simultaneously on one unified dashboard.
